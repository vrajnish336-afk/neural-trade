## 4. Replication Methodology
A hypothesis is not considered "replicated" merely by running the exact same backtest multiple times. The `ReplicationAnalyzer` compares `dataset_id` string hashes alongside `start_time`, `end_time`, and `seed`. Repeated data is downgraded to `SAME_DATASET_REPLAY` or `DIFFERENT_SEED_ONLY`. Time overlaps yield `OVERLAPPING_DATA`. Only entirely distinct validation bounds earn the `UNSEEN_DATA` or `INDEPENDENT_EXPERIMENT` title.

## 5. Generalization Methodology
The analyzer detects diversity across `regime` and `dataset_id` variables. At minimum, multiple regimes or diverse datasets are required to shift a hypothesis from `LIMITED_SCOPE` to `PARTIAL_GENERALIZATION` or `GENERALIZES`.

## 6. Evidence-Strength Methodology
Evidence Strength applies an explicitly deterministic heuristic rule set:
- If a hypothesis triggers a falsification threshold in Phase 34, its Phase 35 strength is irreversibly bounded to `CONFLICTED`.
- If high repetition is detected with no independent unseen boundaries, the evidence strength is `FRAGILE` and flags a `MULTIPLE_TESTING_RISK`.
- A classification of `STRONG` mandates at least 2 independent validation units AND broad generalization scope.
