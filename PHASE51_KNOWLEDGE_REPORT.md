# PHASE 51 KNOWLEDGE REPORT

## 1. Established Knowledge
Claims reaching `ESTABLISHED_WITHIN_BOUNDARY` signify independent reproducibility (3+ successful validations) under tightly controlled single-factor isolations. The architecture formally bridges Evidence, Reproduction, Discrepancy, and Phase 50 Revalidation into a canonized intelligence layer.

## 2. Conditional Knowledge
Claims labeled `CONDITIONALLY_SUPPORTED` explicitly inherit their Regime, Timeframe, Methodology, and Cost boundaries. Generalization outside these bounds generates a Phase 32 Research Gap. 

## 3. Weakened Knowledge
`WEAKENED` knowledge occurs strictly when Phase 50 Revalidation determines that Discrepancy Isolation structurally diverges from original results, effectively scaling back the initial hypothesis confidence.

## 4. Conflicted Knowledge
`CONFLICTED` states arise when equal sets of independent evidence return opposing outcomes (Phase 37 Consensus logic ported via Phase 50).

## 5. Insufficient Evidence
Claims with < 3 isolated confirmations or unresolved interactions return `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## 6. Recurring Discrepancy Patterns
The `KnowledgePattern` mechanism formally detects recurrence (e.g. `PatternFamily.DATASET`). If the same dataset repeatedly produces conflicted validations, the dataset is marked as a structural sensitivity pattern.

## 7. Recurring Structural Strengths
Patterns of consistent independent replication (e.g. regime resiliency).

## 8. Recurring Structural Weaknesses
Cost-sensitivity loops, single-timeframe degradation, or high discrepancy counts.

## 9. Evidence Gaps
Generated as `KnowledgeGap` objects requiring `INDEPENDENT_DATASET_TEST`.

## 10. Unresolved Interactions
Flagged when OFAT (Phase 49) reports `INTERACTION_UNRESOLVED`.

## 11. Research Hypotheses
Negative tests flow back as gaps without modifying history.

## 12. Future Research Questions
Output as deterministic constraints, sent to Phase 32.

## 13. Evidence Lineage
Preserved via `source_experiment_ids` and ID hashing.

## 14. Generalization Boundaries
Always stated explicitly: "Within the tested datasets..."

## 15. Temporal Limitations
Decay loops enforce staleness based on Phase 36 aging.

## 16. Methodology Limitations
`methodology_version` stamps ensure that knowledge synthesis changes track the algorithm version, preventing retroactive contamination.
